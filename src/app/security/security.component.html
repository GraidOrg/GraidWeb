How Graid connects to Canvas

Graid integrates with Canvas using LTI 1.3 / LTI Advantage, the standard higher-ed institutions already trust for third-party tools.

  • No password sharing. Launches use OpenID Connect. Students and instructors are authenticated by Canvas, and Graid never sees or stores LMS passwords.
  • Least-privilege data. Graid receives only the roster and context the launch provides for the course it's placed in. confirm which roster/NRPS fields you request
  • Grades flow back through the standard. Scores post to your gradebook via Assignment & Grade Services (AGS), the same mechanism Canvas uses for any compliant tool.
  • Institution-controlled. An admin installs and can remove Graid at any time; access ends when the placement is removed.

Student data and FERPA

What we collect: student name and course context from the LMS launch, and the content of tutoring sessions the student has with Graid (see Recorded sessions below). confirm the exact list

What we don't do: we don't sell student data, we don't use student content to train third-party public models, and we don't share it with anyone outside the instructional context. confirm each

FERPA. Graid operates as a school official with a legitimate educational interest under FERPA, acting under the institution's direction. Student records are used only to deliver tutoring and instructor visibility for that course. confirm this is your intended contractual basis

Retention & deletion. Session data is retained for confirm period and deleted on request. Institutions can request export or deletion of their data by contacting us.
